# QUID inclusion markers: words introducing a share of the parent in a
# percentage declaration, e.g. "meat (36%, of which 20% beef)",
# "viandes (36%, dont 4% de bœuf)", "Fleisch (30%, davon 20% Rind)".
#
# The ingredient parser drops a marker directly followed by a percentage so
# that the share is parsed like any percent-prefix ingredient ("20% beef").
# A marker NOT followed by a percentage is left alone: nutrition remarks
# ("dont sucres", "of which saturates", "waarvan suikers") are untouched.

en: of which, including, incl.
da: hvoraf
de: davon
es: de los cuales, de las cuales, de la cual
fi: josta
fr: dont
it: di cui
nb: hvorav
nl: waarvan
pl: w tym
pt: dos quais, das quais, do qual, da qual
sv: varav
