Siza ukubhadela isabelomali se-Open Food Facts sango-2026!

I-Open Food Facts imahala nge-100% begodu ayikho ngaphasi kwekampani zokudla. Sitlhoga irhelebho lakho bona siragele phambili sikhulise iphrojekthi le.

Kubayini unikele ku-Open Food Facts?

I-Open Food Facts iyi-"Wikipedia yokudla", isilulu sedatha esikhulu samahala nesivulekileko semikhiqizo yokudla ephasini loke. Sathoma i-Open Food Facts ngombana sikholelwa bona ilwazi malungana nalokho esikudlako limsebenzi womphakathi begodu kufuze libe sesidlangalaleni, limahala begodu livuleke.

Nekhe sithoma i-Open Food Facts, sakhetha ngokukhanyako ukuba yihlangano engenzi inzuzo bona sikhulise amandla nomthelela wethu ngokunikela ngomsebenzi wethu woke simahala. Sakhetha godu ngokukhanyako bona sizokuzimela ngokupheleleko kwekampani zokudla, begodu angeze samukele nanyana ngiliphi isizo leemali elingabeka umnqopho wethu engozini.

Sitlhoga irhelebho lakho bona siragele phambili nokusebenza nokukhulisa i-Open Food Facts. Sibawa ucabange ngokunikela ngomnikelo ukuvikela nokusekela i-Open Food Facts.

Abantu namaphrojekthi: Ukunikela isisekelo sethu esiphezulu emphakathini we-Open Food Facts, ukusebenzisana nabacwaningi, nokwenza idatha yethu yemikhiqizo yokudla ibe nomthelela omkhulu epilweni yethu, ephasini lethu, nasemphakathini.
