Do you know how much sugar is hiding in everyday food products?

Test your intuition by guessing the equivalent number of 4g sugar cubes!

To see the sugar cubes fall, you need a modern web browser.

Why this game?

"How much sugar?" helps everyone—children and adults alike—visualize how much sugar is contained in everyday drinks, breakfast cereals, yogurts, snacks, and savory sauces by comparing them with standard 4g sugar cubes.

For instance, a single 330 ml can of soda often packs the equivalent of nearly 9 sugar cubes (35 g of sugar), exceeding the World Health Organization's ideal daily recommendation of 25 g of free sugars in one go.

Data source: Open Food Facts

All food product data and photos come from the collaborative, free, and open Open Food Facts database. The data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL) and the photos under Creative Commons Attribution Share-Alike.

Use the "Search / Test" tab at the top of the page to search any product or barcode live from the Open Food Facts database!
